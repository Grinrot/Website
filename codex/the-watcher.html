The Watcher

Location: Hospital Room

Classification: Sedation Event / Unidentified Observer

Status: Unresolved

Witness Account

The straps were tight enough that I could feel my pulse beating against them.

A nurse told me the sedative would help me rest. I tried to ask how long I’d be there, but the words came slowly, thick and useless. She said something reassuring. I couldn’t hold on to it.

The door was open a few inches. Through it, I saw someone standing in the hall.

I thought it was another nurse. Then the nurse beside me checked the monitor and left the room without looking toward the door.

The person in the hall didn’t move.

The medicine pulled the ceiling away from me. The person stayed sharp. Tall. Head tilted slightly, as if listening.

I tried to call out. My tongue wouldn’t work.

The figure stepped into the room.

It didn’t walk like someone crossing a floor. One moment it was by the door; the next it was beside the bed. The straps held my arms down. I could only watch its face lean toward mine.

It had no features I could make out. Just a pale, smooth blur where a face should have been.

I tried to shut my eyes. They wouldn’t stay closed.

The figure watched me fight the sedative. When my eyelids drooped, it leaned closer. When I forced them open, it drew back a little.

It was waiting for me to fall asleep.

I heard shoes in the hallway. The nurse came back and adjusted something near my arm. She didn’t react to the figure standing over me. Didn’t even glance at it.

The nurse said, “You’re all right. Just let yourself rest.”

The figure bent until its blank face hovered inches above mine.

Then, from somewhere inside that smooth blur, I heard my own voice whisper:

“Don’t let them put me under.”

The nurse’s hand pressed gently against my shoulder.

And the room went dark.
