CODEX 023 · RECOVERED DOCUMENT

Noise Alert

Location: Private Residence

Classification: Nocturnal Entity / Vital Extraction

Status: Unresolved

Witness Account

Every morning, his watch showed the same notification: Noise alert.

He blamed his wife’s snoring.

“Another night of you trying to start a chainsaw,” he’d tease her. She’d throw a pillow at him, and he’d head out for his walk.

On Thursday, he checked the bedroom camera to see what sound had set it off. The alert was timestamped 3:17 a.m.

He pressed play.

The room was still. He and his wife slept beneath the blankets. Then something darker than the dark lowered over her side of the bed.

It hovered above her, shapeless and silent.

A thin, silver strand slipped from her open mouth. The mass drew it in slowly, like it was drinking her breath. Her face grew slack. The hand resting on the blanket went limp.

He leaned closer to the screen.

The mass stopped.

A face pushed out of it—pale, stretched, staring straight into the lens. It lunged.

The screen broke into static.

He ran upstairs. His wife was asleep, breathing softly. He shook her awake. She blinked at him, annoyed, and asked what was wrong.

That night, he stayed awake beside her.

At 3:17, his watch chimed.

Noise alert.

The room was silent.

Then, from the camera speaker, came a slow, wet inhale.
