CODEX 024 · RECOVERED DOCUMENT

Chime

Location: Private Residence

Classification: Unverified Intrusion / Anomalous Alert

Status: Unresolved

Witness Account

His phone chimed.

A person is standing inside the house.

“Weird,” he thought, and put the phone down.

Hours later, his phone chimed again.

A person is standing inside the house.

Annoyed, he opened the doorbell camera app. Then he remembered: the camera faced away from the house.

A cold chill passed over him.

His phone chimed.

A person is standing inside the house.
